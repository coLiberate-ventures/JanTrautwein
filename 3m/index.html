The rules of the game

Same rhythm every week. Your own game inside it.

This isn't a course with modules. It's a container. You bring your business, your offer and your goals. And besides the money, something even more exciting happens: you level up as a character. You reach new levels by upgrading your belief system, and one of the best ways to do that is through new experiences you create by taking action.

Take action→New experiences→New beliefs→Next level

You're in the right place if

  • You'd make money at any cost, including your integrity.
  • You want to learn about money without doing anything differently.

Questions

Before you apply

I don't have my offer fully figured out. Can I still join?

Yes. You need to know roughly what you want to sell. If the offer needs work, we shape it together in the first weeks so you can start selling it.

What if the others make much more money than me?

That's completely fine. Everyone plays their own game. Going from €3,000 to €6,000 a month means you doubled your income, and that's a huge win. We count Money Moves, not amounts, and we celebrate every level up.

Do I have to share my numbers?

We share before and after numbers because celebrating progress is part of the game. It's a safe space: no ranking, no judging, and nobody is compared to anyone else. If it feels uncomfortable at first, we go at your pace.

Can my game be about working less?

Yes. Some players want to make more money. Others want to make the same money in fewer hours. Both are great games to play here.

What if I miss a call?

Every call is recorded. You can watch it back and talk to your AI about what was discussed, so nothing gets lost.

How much time does it take?

Plan for the 90 minute call each week plus your daily Money Moves. Most moves are small actions you can do in a focused part of your day.

Do I need to be good with AI?

No. We show you how to use it for the things that help you make money, like reviewing your sales calls and remembering everything from our sessions.
