# Stone and bronze

Direction selected and refined with Jan on 10 October 2026. Main website should be mostly light and spacious. Journey keeps its own established adventure expression. The main palette no longer depends on teal imagery.

## Color roles

1. Foundation: #F3F1EB
2. Quiet surfaces: #E8E6E1
3. Main text: #191B1E
4. Secondary text: #555752
5. Dividers: #D3D0C9
6. Primary button background: #BE9160
7. Primary button text: #0F1113
8. Primary button hover: #D8AE80
9. Bronze text accents on light surfaces: #8A5F2C
10. Dark section background: #0F1113
11. Text on dark surfaces: #E2E0DA

## Typography

Lora is the personal website heading typeface. Manrope is the body and navigation typeface. Grenze is the adventurous display typeface inside Journey, with Spectral body copy and Chivo Mono interface labels.

## Imagery

Use jan-bronze.png for the current hero. Keep its transparent painted edges. Future image direction can use natural skin tones, warm light, stone, and charcoal. New testimonial portraits are still being located and reviewed.

## Motion

Subtle landscape parallax, natural page scrolling, and no abrupt palette transformations. Respect reduced motion preferences.
