How it works

Simple by design.

What does the engagement look like?

We start with a fit call. If there is a real fit, we shape a private coaching engagement around the season you are in, with regular deep sessions and direct support between them where useful.

Is this business consulting?

Sometimes we will look at the business. The deeper value is how you see, decide and lead inside it. That tends to change everything else.

Who is this not for?

People looking for a rigid playbook, a hype man, or someone to outsource their decisions to. You bring the responsibility. I bring candour, perspective and a room where you can think.
