A perspective I love

Life as a role playing game.

We all have ways of making sense of life. I find this one particularly useful. You can see yourself as both the character living the story and the player who can step back and choose a direction.

Your life is real. Your challenges matter. And sometimes a different perspective opens up possibilities you couldn't see before.

01 / Perspective

See the bigger story.

You can zoom out from the situation in front of you and see the chapter you're living. What has shaped you? What matters now? What kind of experience do you want to create?

A question to sit withWhat would you notice if you could zoom out?

02 / Main quest

Choose a quest that matters.

You can bring more intention to where your attention goes. Your main quest might be building a business, creating a home, deepening a relationship, or bringing something beautiful into the world.

A question to sit withWhat feels worth giving yourself to in this chapter?

03 / Skill tree

Grow into your next chapter.

You can get clear on the skills and qualities your next chapter asks of you. Courage. Communication. Focus. Leadership. Your time is limited, so it helps to choose what you want to practise.

A question to sit withWhat would you love to become more capable of?

04 / Challenges

Turn experience into growth.

You can meet a challenge with curiosity. A difficult conversation, a setback, or an old pattern can show you where there is something to learn. You can try, reflect, adjust, and see the progress you're making.

A question to sit withWhat might this challenge be inviting you to learn?

05 / Play

Enjoy being alive.

You can care deeply about your life and still hold it a little more lightly. Try things. Follow your curiosity. Celebrate a small win. Leave room for the unexpected. There is more to the adventure than reaching the next level.

A question to sit withWhere could you bring a little more play into your life?

Who I Am

Outside the mainstream.

I've never had a traditional job.

The “normal” path never felt enticing to me. I'd rather create my own way than follow a path just because everyone else is on it. I'm a bit of a free rebel in that way.

After I finished my physics degree, I cofounded a social startup. It was exciting and fun… until it wasn't.

The biggest realization I had after I left: Happiness has to be the fuel, not the reward.

In 2018 I found my passion in supporting others through deep 1:1 work and dedicated myself fully to it. Since then I've worked with over 100 clients across the world.

I've always loved games, adventures, nature and personal growth. Seeing life as an RPG brings those worlds together for me. There is something freeing about taking your life seriously while also remembering that you get to play.

I keep investing in my own development, through coaching, retreats, and the everyday practice of living what I learn.

I'm based in Lisbon with my wife, Diara, and our two cats, Luno and Koda. I play lots of different sports. I read a lot. And I love a good adventure.

How I Work

Deep, focused, 1:1.

I work with a small number of clients at any given time. We go deep and far together, over several months, usually with two or three sessions a month and support in between.

We start with you. What you want to create, where you are now, and what feels difficult. Sometimes that means getting clear on your next move. Sometimes it means looking at the beliefs and patterns shaping the way you see yourself.

I bring questions, perspective, honest reflection, and the kind of support that helps you follow through. We turn what you see in our conversations into something you can actually live.

The RPG perspective is one of the lenses we can use. Our conversations follow what is useful for you.

Sessions happen over video call or over audio while we're both outside walking in nature. The best conversations don't always happen sitting down.

When I work with someone, I'm all in to support them, their mission and their life. So I choose my clients as carefully as they choose me.
