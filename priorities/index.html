What comes first?

You cannot give everything first place. Put the ten areas of life in your order, notice where your time disagrees, and choose the trade you are willing to make.

Inspired by Alex Hormozi’s reflections on commitment, choice and the fact that every yes quietly contains a no.

Choose your season

Build a life you chose.

This is not a scorecard for a “balanced” life. Your order can change. The point is to decide consciously, rather than letting expired options and other people’s preferences decide for you.

Step one

Put them in your order.

Drag the cards, or use the arrow buttons. Do not rank what sounds admirable. Rank what you genuinely want to make important in this season.

Saved on this device

Step two

Does your life agree?

For each area, estimate the attention it actually receives now. Think time, energy, care and money, not just good intentions.

0 = almost none · 10 = a great deal

Step three

Make the trade visible.

Every added hour comes from somewhere. A clean trade is kinder than a vague promise to somehow do more of everything.

←

Step four

Find the richer move.

Alex’s practical idea: look for actions that serve several priorities at once. Not as a trick to have it all, but as intelligent design.

One action, several boxes

Describe an activity that could nourish two or more of your priorities, then choose the areas it serves.

Choose at least two areas.

Keep the choice close.
