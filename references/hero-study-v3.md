# Hero study 03

The credibility sentence ends with a semicolon. The separate logo pause button is removed. Global Pause movement remains available.

The headline now reads: Create something meaningful. Enjoy your life along the way.

An opaque paper silhouette sits underneath the fading portrait. The rings and orbiting planets can no longer show through the sweatshirt fade. Both layers use the same portrait asset and matching contain geometry.

The preview selector offers four treatments:

1. Soft flip: each word turns in three dimensional space.
2. Vertical glide: the current word lifts away and the next arrives from below. This is the default.
3. Quiet dissolve: words fade out and in without directional movement.
4. Letter cascade: letters arrive in a staggered sequence with a small upward turn.

All modes respect reduced motion and the global pause control. The selector is for design review and is not intended for the final website.

The prior working preview is preserved in the canonical website archive as hero study v2. Nothing was published.
