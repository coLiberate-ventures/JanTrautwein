# My Story image provenance

The new story page uses the chapter image sets from `DEPLOY/images/timeline`. Those sets already appear on the current live story page. The unmodified originals remain in `DEPLOY` and in the older `AI Website/AI Deploy/Timeline` folders.

`work/build-story.mjs` creates smaller JPEG copies for the new local page at `site/story/assets` and the matching preview copies in `outputs/story/assets`. It orders each chapter's images by the existing numbered filenames. It does not alter or remove the source photos.

The chapter copy was adapted from the current `jantrautwein.com/story.html`. It is provisional and should be reviewed by Jan before publishing, especially the personal details in the inner work and future chapters.
